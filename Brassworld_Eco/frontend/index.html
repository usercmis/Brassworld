Eco rules & scenario limits

Sell: cotton +1, manufactured goods +2, pottery +3 pollution (maximum 10). Build at pollution 6+: £5 extra. Clean Up: £5 + one action, reduce pollution by 3 in a city with your industry. Eco Upgrade: £8 + one action, reduce an unsold sales industry's emissions by 2 (minimum 0); earn 2 green points when sold. Each green point becomes 1 VP at final scoring. All actions discard one demo card.
